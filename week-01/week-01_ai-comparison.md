# Week 01 AI Assistant Comparison

## Common Prompt

> "Explain the difference between Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI to a beginner engineer, and provide an everyday example for each."

## Tool 1: ChatGPT (GPT-4o)

* **Answer Summary:** Explained the relationship between ML, DL, and GenAI using a simple concentric-circle analogy. ML was presented as learning patterns from data, DL as using multi-layer neural networks, and GenAI as creating new content.
* **Strengths:** Very well organized, easy to understand, and supported by simple analogies that made the concepts accessible.
* **Weaknesses:** The explanation was introductory and did not cover different kinds of limitations.

## Tool 2: Gemini

* **Answer Summary:** Clearly described the three concepts and emphasized differences in the type and amount of data commonly used, along with practical everyday examples.
* **Strengths:** Good technical differentiation, especially when explaining structured and unstructured data, with concise supporting points.
* **Weaknesses:** The information was somewhat more compact and technically dense compared with ChatGPT.
